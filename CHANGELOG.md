## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-21

First release.

### Added

- Joining the camera's Wi-Fi from the app, reconnecting to the last camera on launch, and a Quick Settings tile that opens the remote.
- Gallery of the photos on the card, with full-size JPEG and NEF saving.
- Sharing as the original files, JPEG, HEIF or WebP copies (size, crop, optional photo card), a ZIP or a contact-sheet PDF, with location removal.
- Remote: live view, shooting with or without autofocus, touch and manual focus, bulb, movies and exposure settings.
- Property inspector with readable names and values.
- Learn: lessons on aperture, shutter speed, ISO, exposure, white balance and framing, "Explain a photo", and a Recipes chapter with before/after previews.
